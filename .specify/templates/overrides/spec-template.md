  Assign priorities (P1, P2, P3, etc.) to each story, where P1 is the most critical.
  Think of each story as a standalone slice of functionality that can be:
  - Developed independently
  - Tested independently
  - Deployed independently
  - Demonstrated to users independently

  SCENARIO IDENTIFIERS (constitution Principle III): every acceptance scenario carries the
  identifier [###]-US<story>-AS<scenario>, where [###] is the number of this feature's spec
  directory (e.g., 003-US1-AS2). Tests name the scenario they cover by this identifier.
  Once the feature is merged, identifiers are never renumbered or reused: a new scenario is
  appended to its story with the next number, and a removed scenario leaves a gap.

  CRITICAL JOURNEY: answer "Yes" only for a journey that spans the whole product and whose
  failure would leave the product unusable for its main purpose. Such a story gets one
  end-to-end test for its main path; every other story is verified at lower levels.
